Workshop Topics We Offer

Pick from our catalog of workshop themes, or contact us to design a custom program tailored to your students' needs.

🤖

Humanoid Robotics

Program humanoid robots (Unitree, EngineAI) to walk, balance, and perform tasks. From basics to advanced RL training.

🧠

AI Model Training

Train and deploy AI models — from image classification to reinforcement learning for robotics decision-making.

👀

Computer Vision

OpenCV, YOLO, and real-time object detection. Teach robots to see and understand their environment.

🛨

Drone Programming

Autonomous flight, obstacle avoidance, swarm coordination. Program Tello and custom drone platforms.

📡

ROS System

Robot Operating System fundamentals — nodes, topics, services, and building complete ROS-based robotics applications.

⚙

Mechanical Engineering

Design, build, and iterate. From CAD to 3D printing to assembly — the engineering foundations behind every robot.
