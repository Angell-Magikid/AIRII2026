Mission & Vision

Technology Has No Age Limit

We believe in the power of magical young minds. We believe in humanity. We believe in a future where technology grows with humanity — not apart from it.

AIRII exists to empower human-centered technology, promote lifelong learning, and foster innovation at any age. We connect children, adults, and seniors through artificial intelligence, robotics, and cross-generational collaboration.

As REC Foundation's Global Content Partner, we design the competition content, build the curriculum, and train the educators that make AI robotics accessible to every school and community.

Our Story

From MAGIKID to AIRII

What began as Magikid — a beloved robotics education brand serving families in China and the United States — has evolved into AIRII, a global institute for AI robotics innovation. The name changed; the mission deepened.

2013 — 2018
The Magikid Era

Magikid launches robotics education programs and mobile apps for children. Builds a loyal following in China with hands-on robotics camps, coding classes, and STEM events. Wins multiple national-level robotics championships.

2019 — 2021
Magikid EmpowerTech Foundation

Established as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit — Magikid EmpowerTech Foundation — to expand access to AI and robotics education. Launches the "Age-Generated Intelligence" initiative, bridging generations through technology.

2022 — 2024
Going Global

Programs expand to Australia and the United States. Students win national championships across three countries. Partnerships formed with NASA (Space Apps Challenge, JPL, Houston Space Center), Stanford (BEHAVIOR Challenge), Caltech, UCLA RoMeLa Lab, and UCI Robot Ecology Lab.

2025 — Present
AIRII is Born

Magikid EmpowerTech Foundation formally becomes AIRII — AI Robotics Innovation Institute. Named REC Foundation Global Content Partner. Launches the AI Robotics World Championship. Ranked TOP 18 globally in AI education — the only K-12 institution on the list, alongside NVIDIA and Huawei.

Designing the Content That Powers Global Competitions

As REC Foundation's Global Content Partner, AIRII plays a central role in the global robotics competition ecosystem:

🏆
Competition Content Design

We design the AI-driven game challenges, rules, and scoring systems used in REC-sanctioned events.

📚
Curriculum Development

We build the structured learning pathways — 6,000+ courses — that prepare teams for competition.

🎓
Teacher Training & Certification

We certify the coaches and educators who lead teams — ensuring consistent quality worldwide.
