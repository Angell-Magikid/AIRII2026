Three Programs. One Mission.

三大项目 · 同一使命

Your generosity fuels three distinct initiatives that bring AI robotics education to those who need it most.

您的慷慨捐赠支持三大公益项目，将AI机器人教育带给最需要的人。

Youth Charity Activities

青少年公益活动组织

Organizing free robotics workshops, community AI literacy events, and STEM outreach programs for underserved youth in local communities across North America.

为北美各地社区的弱势青少年组织免费机器人工作坊、社区AI素养活动和STEM推广项目。

Funds: Workshop materials · Venue · Instructor stipends · Robot kits

Education Support for Children with Special Medical Needs

特殊疾病青少年儿童教育活动支持

Providing tailored AI robotics education programs, hospital-based learning kits, and adaptive technology workshops for children battling chronic or life-threatening illnesses.

为患有慢性或重大疾病的儿童提供定制化AI机器人教育项目、医院学习套件和适应性技术工作坊。

Funds: Adaptive robot kits · Hospital program licensing · Online learning access · Family support
资金用途：适应性机器人套件 · 医院项目授权 · 在线学习平台 · 家庭支持

🤖

Boundless AIRII — International AI Robotics Hackathon

无界AIRII — 国际AI机器人创新黑客松

An international hackathon bringing together student teams from around the world to solve real-world challenges with AI and robotics. Funding covers travel grants, hardware, judges, and venue.

汇聚全球学生团队的国际黑客松，用AI和机器人技术解决真实世界挑战。资金用于差旅补助、硬件、评委和场地。

Funds: Travel grants for underrepresented teams · Hardware kits · Venue · Prize pool
资金用途：弱势团队差旅补助 · 硬件套件 · 场地 · 奖金池

Tax-Deductible Donation

可抵税捐赠

AIRII (formerly Magikid EmpowerTech Foundation) is a registered 501(c)(3) tax-exempt nonprofit organization (EIN available upon request). All donations are tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by U.S. law. You will receive an automated tax receipt via email after your donation.

AIRII（前身为Magikid EmpowerTech Foundation）是注册的501(c)(3)免税非营利组织（EIN可索取）。所有捐赠均按美国法律最大程度抵税。捐赠后您将收到自动发送的抵税收据邮件。
