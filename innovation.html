How It Works

From application to showcase — here's the journey of an Innovator Club student.

Step 1
Apply

Submit your project idea or area of interest. Open to students in Grades 9-12 (exceptional younger students considered).

Step 2
Selection & Screening

Our team reviews applications and selects students based on passion, project feasibility, and prior experience. Interview may be required.

Step 3
Mentor Matching

Selected students are matched with mentors from Stanford, Caltech, NASA, UCLA, UCI, or industry partners based on project domain.

Step 4
Incubation

3-6 months of guided project development with weekly mentor meetings, access to hardware and compute resources, and peer review sessions.

Step 5
Showcase & Beyond

Present at the AIRII Innovation Showcase. Top projects enter competitions, get published, or continue as ongoing research.
