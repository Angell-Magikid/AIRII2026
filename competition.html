About the Championship

AIRII Designs the Games.
Students Build the Future.

The AI Robotics World Championship is the premier competition for student AI robotics teams. As REC Foundation's Global Content Partner, AIRII is responsible for:

🎮
Game Design

Designing AI-driven challenges that test programming, engineering, and strategic thinking.

📝
Rules & Game Manual

Writing and publishing the official rulebook, scoring criteria, and game specifications.

⚖
Judging & Refereeing

Training certified judges and maintaining competition integrity across all events.

🏆
Championship Oversight

Managing the qualification pathway from regional events to the world championship.

Past Championships

A Legacy of Champions

2025 Season
AI Robotics World Championship 2025

48 teams competed in the inaugural championship. Featured AI-driven autonomous navigation, vision-based object manipulation, and multi-robot coordination challenges.

Winners: Team CyberLions (USA), Team RoboEagles (China)

2024 Season
National Championships 2024

National-level competitions held in China, Australia, and the United States. 100+ state and regional titles awarded across all three countries.

4th consecutive year of national championship wins

2023 Season
NASA Space Apps Challenge Collaboration

AIRII teams collaborated with NASA on the Space Apps Challenge, applying AI robotics to space exploration problems. JPL and Houston Space Center partnerships.

Multiple teams received NASA recognition
